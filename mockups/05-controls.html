Thumb zones

GO and STOP sit inside the natural sweep of each thumb when the tablet is held at the sides. Nothing else is placed in those corners, so a wild jab cannot hit the wrong thing.

Why so far apart

Opposite corners, 780 px apart. GO and STOP can never be confused for one another, and he never has to aim.
