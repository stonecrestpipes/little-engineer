The engine

Stubby, big-wheeled, and mostly face. Turned to camera so you always see it looking at you.

The face is the engine. It is turned toward the camera, not pasted on a profile, so wherever it is on the loop it is looking roughly at him.

Half the length, double the wheels. Real proportions were the problem. A toy is short, tall and sits on wheels too big for it.

Still just primitives. Cylinders, discs and a lathe — nothing here needs a modelling tool.

Three faces
driving
whistling
arriving

Eyes are a third of the face wide, with pupils that drift toward whatever he should be looking at. That is the whole emotional vocabulary — it never speaks.
