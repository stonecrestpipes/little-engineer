The railway

One closed loop. Six things to arrive at. It never ends.

Rhythm

Six events evenly spaced means something happens every 15 seconds. Close enough that the gaps never feel empty, far enough apart that the journey between them still counts as driving.

Why a loop

There is no finish, so there is nothing to lose and no “again?” to negotiate. He stops when he is done, and the tablet can be taken away mid-lap without interrupting anything.

Built to grow

Each marker is one reusable piece. Phase 5 extends the loop outward from the Harbour without touching anything already working.

Not in this version

Junctions · points · signals to obey · reversing · a second train on the line · anything that can go wrong.
